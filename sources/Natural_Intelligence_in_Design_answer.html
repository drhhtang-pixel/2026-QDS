(C2) Cognition & Media

The role of sketching in design

  • Sketches are not only representations as communication aids but also thinking and reasoning aids.
  • Sketches are part of the exploratory process.
  • Stimuli to develop ideas.
  • Media for exploration.
  • Sketches enable designers to handle different levels of abstraction simultaneously.
  • Reflect upon critical details through moving freely between levels
  • Recall relevant knowledge.
  • Assist structuring ill-defined problem
  • Sketches promote the recognition of emergent features and properties of solutions.
  • Sketches are a kind of intelligence amplifier.
Others: Purcell & Gero (1998). Drawings and the design process. Design Studies 19: 389-430.
